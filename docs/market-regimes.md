# Market Regimes

The report groups each completed trade by the point-in-time relationship between EMA(50) and EMA(200) on the trigger timeframe at entry: `BULL_TREND`, `BEAR_TREND`, `TRANSITION`, or `WARMUP_UNAVAILABLE`. It persists count, net P&L and win rate by regime.

The labels do not use future candles; they are an interpretable trend breakdown rather than a retrospective regime classifier.
